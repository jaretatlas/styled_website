## **Project Requirements:**
#### **1. Pages to Include:**
Your portfolio website should consist of the following 4 pages:

- **Home Page**: Introduce yourself and provide a brief overview of the website.
- **Personal Interests**: Share information about your hobbies, passions, or any topics of interest.
- **Media Page**: Showcase images and videos related to your interests or projects.
- **Contact Page**: Include a form or contact details where visitors can reach you.
#### **2. Styling, Layout & Responsiveness:**
- **Responsiveness** – Your site must adapt to various screen sizes (mobile, tablet, desktop).
- **Layout Techniques** – Use **CSS Flexbox** or **CSS Grid** (or both) to build responsive, well-aligned layouts.
- **Consistent Aesthetic** – Choose a **color palette**, **font combination**, and **spacing system**. Apply consistently across pages. You should add three or four colors as the main colors for your website
- **Creative Styling** 
  - A custom-styled navigation menu
  - A CSS transition or animation (hover, fade-in, etc.)
  - A creatively styled button or interactive component
